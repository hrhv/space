Product Managers have the unique role of driving a product from concept to launch and beyond. It requires a broad range of skills to successfully manage a product lifecycle. Here are the top 10 skills needed for a Product Management role:

1. Vision: Product Managers need to have a clear vision for the product and be able to articulate it effectively to the team.
    
2. Product Strategy: Product Managers need to have a deep understanding of the competitive landscape, customer needs, and how to create a product that meets those needs.
    
3. Technical Knowledge: Product Managers need to be familiar with the technical aspects of the product they are managing. They need to understand the architecture, design, and development processes.
    
4. Communication: Product Managers need to be able to communicate effectively with both internal and external stakeholders.
    
5. Collaboration: Product Managers need to be effective collaborators who can work with a variety of teams, including Engineering, Design, Marketing, and Support.
    
6. Problem-solving: Product Managers need to be able to identify and solve problems quickly, efficiently, and effectively.
    
7. Analytical Skills: Product Managers need to have strong analytical skills to be able to interpret data and make informed decisions.
    
8. Leadership: Product Managers need to have the ability to lead teams and motivate them to reach their goals.
    
9. Creativity: Product Managers need to be able to come up with creative solutions to complex problems.
    
10. Time Management: Product Managers need to be able to manage their time effectively, set deadlines, and meet them.
    

These are just some of the skills that are needed for a successful Product Manager role. With these skills, you can be sure to build great products and drive successful product launches.
