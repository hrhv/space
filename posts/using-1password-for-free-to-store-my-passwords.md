When I initially used 1Password at work, I was surprised by the ease with which I can store, retrieve, fill and even share my passwords in case I want to. This was a big shift from my traditional way of

1. storing them in an Excel Workbook;
![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645599425835/M9P5ofJ_d.png)

2. securing the file with a master password (and Touch ID);
![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645599543100/a0wkVMCaV.png)

3. with dozens of different sheets categorising my credentials;
![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645599667867/f6VdqF59V.png)

4. keeping the file in sync with my smartphone through iCloud;
![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645599827872/eR_dXovuj.png)

It was a lot of work doing all of that, honestly.

1Password was pretty good at what it did, but with the [pricing structure](https://1password.com/sign-up/) that they offered for personal and family use, I wasn't sure if it was even worth the $36 or $60 I'd be spending "just to store my passwords".
![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645600789620/pAv_LKOer.png)

I recently learnt that 1Password started offering [free Family Plans to users who use 1Password at work (with a Business plan)](https://support.1password.com/link-family/), and fortunately my organisation is on one of these plans.


![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645601079237/Txo82G_Ok.png)


## Well, so what next?


![image.png](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1645601179965/fjtitVvDZ.png)

To experience the full potential of 1Password, I'm migrating all my passwords (stored at various distributed locations - excel workbook, chrome, safari etc.) to my vaults.

**The one thing which excites me about 1Password (for now) is how seamless the experience is - it has native Mac app, Windows app, Android app, iOS app, a chrome extension and even a Command Line Utility (😳).**

Looking forward to have a better experience now!
