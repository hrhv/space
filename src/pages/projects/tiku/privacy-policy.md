> **The short version.** Tiku is a personal, non-commercial project for friends and family. It sees what you send it and whatever you choose to connect, remembers context so it can be useful, and passes data only to the services it needs in order to work, like the AI models that write its replies. Your data is never sold, never used for ads, and never used by me to train AI models. You can ask me to delete it at any time. The full policy below is what counts.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled by Tiku, a personal AI assistant run by Harshit Budhraja ("I", "me", "my") for invited users ("you") through Telegram, Discord, and any other channel it's made available on. It applies alongside Tiku's [Terms of Service](/projects/tiku/terms-of-service/). This version is effective from the "last updated" date above.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

I am: Harshit Budhraja, an individual based in India, running Tiku as a personal, non-commercial project. Under data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, I'm the "data fiduciary" (also called the "data controller") for the personal data described here. You can reach me at [hello@harshitbudhraja.com](mailto:hello@harshitbudhraja.com).

## 2. What Tiku collects

Tiku only gets what you, or the platforms you use it on, give it:

- **Account details from messaging platforms.** Your user ID, username, display name, and any other profile details Telegram or Discord share with bots, along with the IDs of the chats or channels you talk to Tiku in.
- **What you send.** The messages, voice notes, images, files, and links you share with Tiku, and Tiku's replies to them.
- **What you connect.** If you choose to give Tiku access to another account or service (for example an email inbox, a calendar, or a task list), Tiku can read the data in it, and act on it where you've allowed that, to do what you ask, such as nudging you about an email that needs a reply.
- **Memory.** To be useful over time, Tiku keeps notes, summaries, reminders, and preferences from your conversations. Its memory system, Honcho, also builds an evolving profile of you from those conversations: things like what you're working on, what matters to you, and how you like things done. This profile can include inferences, and those may be wrong.
- **Technical logs.** Timestamps, errors, and usage records produced while Tiku runs, which I use to keep it working.

Tiku doesn't ask for payment details, and doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Please avoid sharing very sensitive information (passwords, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, health details) unless it's genuinely needed for something you've asked Tiku to do.

## 3. How your data is used

Your data is used only to run Tiku for you:

- to understand your requests, reply, and carry out the tasks you ask for;
- to send the nudges, reminders, and scheduled messages you've set up or would reasonably expect;
- to remember context, so Tiku gets more useful over time;
- to troubleshoot, fix, and improve Tiku; and
- to keep Tiku secure, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

I don't routinely read your conversations. I may look at specific conversations, memories, or logs when that's needed to fix a problem, when you ask me to, or when it's required to keep Tiku safe or to comply with the law.

**What your data is never used for:** it isn't sold or rented, it isn't used for advertising, and I don't use it to train AI models.

## 4. Why I'm allowed to process it

Tiku processes your personal data based on your consent, which you give by choosing to use Tiku after being shown this policy and, for anything you connect, by connecting it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by stopping use of Tiku and asking me to delete your data (see section 9). Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before. Where the law requires or allows it, I may also process data to meet a legal obligation or to protect the rights and safety of you, me, or others.

## 5. Who else handles your data

Tiku is a small setup, but it can't work entirely on its own. Your data is shared only with the following, and only as much as each one needs:

- **Messaging platforms.** Your conversations with Tiku pass through Telegram or Discord, which handle them under their own privacy policies ([Telegram](https://telegram.org/privacy), [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy)). Anything you post in a shared space, such as a Discord server or a group chat, is also visible to the other members of that space.
- **AI model providers.** To write replies and carry out tasks, Tiku sends your message and the relevant context (which can include memories and data from connected services) to third-party AI model providers. They process it to generate a response, under their own terms, and some may keep it for a limited time, for example to monitor for abuse.
- **Memory service.** Tiku uses [Honcho](https://honcho.dev/), built by Plastic Labs, to store and reason over its memory of your conversations.
- **Services you connect or ask Tiku to use.** When Tiku acts for you, for instance by checking your inbox or searching the web, that service receives what it needs to do the job.
- **When the law requires it.** I may disclose data if required by law, a court order, or a government authority, or where it's necessary to protect someone's rights, property, or safety.

The providers Tiku uses may change as it evolves. Whichever ones it uses, I'll only pass your data to them for the purposes described in this policy.

## 6. Where your data is stored

Tiku itself runs on hardware I own and operate at home in India: an LXC container on a Proxmox server in my homelab. Some of the providers listed in section 5 may process data on servers in other countries. When data leaves India, it goes only to providers Tiku needs in order to work, and only as applicable law permits.

## 7. How long it's kept

- **Conversations, memory, and data from connected services** are kept while you use Tiku, because that's what lets it remember context.
- **If you ask me to delete your data,** or I end your access, I'll delete it from Tiku and its memory within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed as those backups are rotated out.
- **Technical logs** are kept only as long as they're useful for keeping Tiku running, and are deleted on a rolling basis.
- I'll keep data for longer only where the law requires it.

Data already held by third parties, such as messages in your Telegram chat history or data an AI provider keeps under its own terms, is governed by their policies, though I'll help where I can.

## 8. Keeping it secure

I take reasonable steps to protect your data, such as running Tiku on private infrastructure that I administer myself, keeping the credentials for connected services on that infrastructure and using them only for what you've asked, and using encrypted connections to the services Tiku relies on. Still, no system is perfectly secure, and I can't guarantee your data will never be accessed without authorisation. If a breach affects your personal data, I'll tell you without undue delay, and notify the authorities where the law requires it.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask me to:

- **access** a summary of the personal data Tiku holds about you and how it's used;
- **correct** or update anything that's inaccurate or incomplete;
- **delete** your data, including Tiku's memory of you;
- **withdraw your consent**, and stop using Tiku, at any time;
- **disconnect** any account you've connected to Tiku; and
- **nominate** someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To do any of this, email [hello@harshitbudhraja.com](mailto:hello@harshitbudhraja.com) or message me directly. I may need to confirm it's really you first, and I'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with how I've handled a request, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live; in India, that's the Data Protection Board of India.

## 10. Google user data

This section only applies if you connect a Google account, such as Gmail or Google Calendar. Tiku's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data:

- is used only to provide and improve the features you've asked Tiku for;
- is transferred to others (such as the AI model providers in section 5) only as needed for those features, for security, or to comply with the law;
- is never sold, and never used for advertising;
- is never used to develop, improve, or train generalised AI or machine learning models; and
- isn't read by a person unless you've given permission, it's needed for security purposes or to comply with the law, or it has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

## 11. Children

Tiku is meant for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it, and I don't knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18. If I learn that I have, I'll delete it.

## 12. This website

These web pages use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without cookies and don't identify you personally. This section covers the website only; the rest of this policy covers Tiku itself.

## 13. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy as Tiku changes. When I do, I'll change the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes I'll let you know through Tiku or directly before they take effect. If you keep using Tiku after an update takes effect, you accept the updated policy.

## 14. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests, or complaints about this policy or your data go to me, Harshit Budhraja, at [hello@harshitbudhraja.com](mailto:hello@harshitbudhraja.com). I'm also the person responsible for handling grievances about Tiku. I'll acknowledge every complaint and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
